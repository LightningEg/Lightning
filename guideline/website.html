  • A guide answers one question people search for, such as net worth, a budget in EGP or safe to spend. Its label names the topic (“Net worth tracker for Egypt”), its page title starts with what people search for and ends “· Lightning”.

B08Calculator

Keeping money each month is like owning capital that pays it. The answer sits on the vivid gradient.

What Could You Keep Each Month Without Missing It?
2,000EGP
That’s like having
120,000 EGP

in the bank. At 20% a year, that pays you 2,000 EGP a month, without having the money first.

  • One card split in two: the question and inputs on paper, the answer on the vivid gradient with a white number. This is the one card allowed on the gradient.
  • The answer is the capital that pays what you keep: monthly × 12 ÷ the yearly return. It sits in the centre of the vivid side, up to 96px (46px on phones), with one chip under it: Kept in a year.
  • Starts at 2,000 EGP and 20%. No button and no disclaimer line. The answer reads as money in the bank: "That’s like having 120,000 EGP in the bank. At 20% a year, that pays you 2,000 EGP a month, without having the money first."
  • On A after How it works; on B after the breakfast story, which counts what you keep, not what you stop spending: 150 at the café, 50 at home, 100 kept, × 20 = 2,000 a month.
  • No explanation note and no field hint. If it needs a description, rewrite it.
