Lightning guideline · Phone

Part C is the app on a phone: frame and safe areas, navigation, header, touch targets, cards, charts, lists, sheets, type and screens. On a phone, C wins over A.

A · AppB · WebsiteC · Phone

Three documents, one folder. This is the phone document (the app on a phone). For the app on a PC open app.html, for the website website.html, for the phone phone.html. They share one logo, colours, type and numbers; where they differ, use the document for the surface you are building.

  • C01.1 Direction: a phone is not a small PC. Banking and money apps people open daily (Monzo, Revolut, Wise, Copilot) open on the money that just moved, not on analysis. Each of their screens answers one question, with one figure as the lead and the rest quiet.
  • Money first. Every page that has a ledger leads with the latest transactions; the analysis follows and is a tap away.
  • Flat, not boxed. Rows sit on the page, divided by a hairline. A card is for one lead figure; a list is never inside a card.
  • One figure per row. Name and category left, amount right. Anything more (balance after, account, notes) is in the row's sheet.
  • Quiet chrome. One period control, one set of tabs, no icon boxes, no coloured edges. Colour appears only where it carries meaning (A03).
  • Recon, 2026-10-14: web evidence on these apps is anecdotal (user forums, design critiques), so these rules rest on patterns repeated across them, not on measured studies. Revolut's crowded home with a single visible transaction is the one named failure: show at least five.

C03Navigation

The sidebar becomes a tab bar at the bottom. Two levels still: a section, then a tab.

C03.1Tab bar: the five sections

OverviewBudgetCashInvestmentsAccounts
  • Five slots: Overview, Budget, Cash (Cash planning), Investments, Accounts. A sixth section never gets a slot; it opens from Settings.
  • Each slot: a 24px Lucide icon over an 11px name, always both. Chosen: a Nile pill behind the icon and an Ink name. Others: Muted.
  • Icons say the section: house, wallet, banknote for Cash, chart, bank. The shield belongs to Reserves and the emergency fund, never to a section.
  • Accounts is a section: the desktop's Accounts bar under the menu leaves the top of every page. Its total opens the Accounts tab, in whole EGP.

C03.2App bar

  • Left: the mark alone, 28px, no wordmark. On an inner page, ‹ and the page it returns to (“‹ Accounts”).
  • Right, in this order: Search, Hide amounts, Settings. 44px targets, 22px icons. Search opens full screen; no “Ctrl K” on a phone.
  • The tab's gear moves here from the end of the tab bar (A10.4): Settings opens the current tab's settings first.

C03.3Tabs inside a section

SummarySpendingHealth

Up to three tabs: one row of words, equal widths

PlanRecurringLoansReserves

Four tabs: two rows of two, still words

  • Words, never icons alone: a section's own icons (a bank for Loans, a shield for Reserves) are not universal, so the name is the tab. A name may wrap to two lines; it is never cut.
  • Full width under the period control, each tab 48 tall. The track never scrolls. Five tabs: split the section.
  • Swiping the content left or right does not change tabs: charts and rows need the swipe.

C03.4Going deeper and back

  • An account, a holding or a category opens as a full page with ‹ in the app bar. The tab bar stays, its section still chosen.
  • The phone's back gesture does what ‹ does. From a section's first page it goes to Overview, then leaves the app.

C07Charts

Every chart in A12 has one phone form. Most are redrawn; a few become rows.

C07.1Rules for every chart

  • Full card width, 140 to 180 tall. One chart per card.
  • Three labels on a time axis at most: first, middle, last. No y-axis labels; one line at zero.
  • Never a cut label. If a name does not fit on the mark, the mark loses its label and the name goes in the rows under the chart.
  • Tap or drag to read: the card's figure shows the value under the finger, and returns when you let go. No tooltips.
  • No legend sentence. Colour is explained by the rows, or by one word on each end of a scale.

  • C08.1 Day headers: Today, Yesterday, then the date (A06.1), once, Muted, 12.5px, above that day's rows. Not repeated on each row.
  • C08.2 Row: flat on the page, a hairline between rows, never a card around the list. A 40px round initial (soft green for money in, a neutral well otherwise), the counterparty and its category, the amount on the right. 64 tall. In: green with +. Out: ink with −. Transfers: no sign (A11). The balance after the row shows only on an account's own list, Muted under the amount; never on Overview or the all-accounts list. The Overview names no account (category only); the all-accounts list adds it after the category.
  • C08.3 Filters: search full width (it takes a #tag too), then chips. The month is a chip with ‹ › and opens the month picker; it is never typed as yyyy-mm.
  • C08.4 Adding: the add button (C05.2) opens the entry sheet. No “New” row at the top of the list.
  • C08.5 Selecting: press and hold a row, or the Select chip after the month. Chosen rows turn soft green with a Meadow edge; a bar replaces the tab bar with the count, the category to give them, Change category and Delete. Done ends it.
  • C08.6 Categories: L1 as header with its total, L2s under it, four then Other (A11). Never “L1 › L2”.

  • C09.2 Fields: soft wells as A10.3, 48 tall, the amount 56. Label above, one column, 16px text.

  • C09.6 Profiles: list the profiles and Create a profile. “Import a profile” opens the phone's file picker. No folder paths, no typed database path, no notes under the cards. The line under the title says “on this phone”.

C11Screens

Two pages built only from C02 to C10.

  • C11.1 is the whole Overview, money first: Get set up while it is not done; the net worth figure on the page (the lead, not in a card); Latest, the six newest transactions across every account with “See all transactions”; Needs you; then This period, with its period control and the four tiles; then Your position. Under them, one row each for the rest (Over time, Free cash, What it is made of, If you sold today; then Net flow, Where money in went, Investments, and each month chart), with its figure; a row opens its card in place (C06.2). Analysis is one tap away, never first. Bills due to pay stay open, as their own row.
  • C11.2: the account's one Secondary and ⋯ sit under its figure. On a brokerage, “Record an investment transaction” folds above the list for buy, sell and dividend.
