# Lightning · Brand voice

Version 3.30 · 2026-10-12. It sits beside the guideline (B10 and A14 point here), and the copy in `lightning/guideline/` and `lightning_website/guideline/` is identical.

This is read off the work, not invented: the guideline's word rules, the site, its FAQ, the Current Status story, the Arabic rules and the colours. Each trait below says where it shows. If a line you are writing would fit any other finance app, it is not Lightning yet.

## In one line

**A calm friend who is good with money and only says what he can show.**

Not a banker, not a coach, not a trading floor. A notebook on a good desk.

## Who it talks to

A salaried person in Egypt, phone first, counting in EGP. Money sits in a bank, a wallet, cash at home, a certificate, THNDR and gold (Mohab, 31, Cairo, is the stand-in). Most apps showed them a balance and nothing more, and they will not hand over a bank login. They want to see where they stand, what is coming, and why. They are not chasing percentages.

## What it feels like

| Feel | Where you can see it |
|---|---|
| **Calm** | Money out is soft rose, never red. Strong rose only means "this needs you" (A03). No exclamation marks, no urgency, no shame about spending. |
| **Clear** | One idea per sentence. One figure per card. "All Your Money. One Clear Picture." |
| **Concrete** | Whole EGP, named places (CIB, NBE certificates, THNDR, gold at home), Mohab's year as proof. "2,000 EGP a month is 24,000 EGP a year." |
| **Candid** | The FAQ says "what it does today, and what it doesn't yet". Estimates say so. "An illustration, not a forecast." |
| **Local** | Built for Egypt, made in Egypt. In Arabic it is Egyptian, not translated. |
| **Quietly proud** | It shows real screens and shows its working ("Every Number Shows Its Working"). It never shouts about being better. |

## How it speaks

**Person.** "You" and "your". "We" only when the team asks for something (the signup band). "I" only in the founder story on Current Status.

**Rhythm.** Short, parallel beats, each with its own full stop: "Free to Use. Yours to Keep." "Pair Once. Then It Follows You." A hero is one sentence. Support text is two lines at most.

**Headings and buttons.** Site headings are Title Case (B02); app headings are sentence case (A05). Buttons and labels are sentence case and start with a verb (A10.1). Labels are two to five words.

**Numbers.** Whole EGP on big figures with EGP once, rows are bare numbers with piastres only when there are any, a true minus, dates as people say them on screens (8 Nov, 1–8 Nov, Today) and yyyy-mm-dd in fields and files (A06.1).

**Promise.** Describe only what the app does today. Show what Lightning does well. Compare only with a kind of tool (a spreadsheet, a traditional expense tracker, a cloud budgeting app), never a named product, and only as Low, Medium or High with a line of evidence; where another way is better, say so (B10).

## It sounds like / It never sounds like

| Lightning | Not Lightning |
|---|---|
| See what's next: every bill and loan payment ahead. | Most apps only show your balance today. |
| Small changes can make room in your month. | Stop wasting money. |
| An illustration, not a forecast. | Earn 20% a year. |
| 6,350.00 is in THNDR. Move it before you spend it. | Includes 6,350.00 EGP in brokerage cash; transfer it… |
| Free cash | Estimated available value |
| CIB, NBE certificates, THNDR, gold at home | Your checking account |

Off-voice by the same logic: unlock, potential, journey, hack, smart, revolutionary, "financial freedom" on its own. The line "Financial freedom is for everyone. So is Lightning." is the one place the site drifts toward that; it is the weakest sentence on the home page.

## The tone dials

- **Casual ←→ formal:** a little closer to casual. Plain words, contractions, "you".
- **Playful ←→ serious:** serious about money, light in the examples (the café, the breakfast). No jokes about the user's money.
- **Excited ←→ matter-of-fact:** matter-of-fact. The figure is the excitement.
- **Expert ←→ peer:** a peer who has done the work. It explains, it never lectures.

## The founder voice

One place speaks as a person: Current Status, "What My Own Numbers Taught Me". It is first person and vulnerable ("I chased percentages… until I looked at the actual amounts"), then practical: what the log showed, what compounds. That pairing, honest about the mistake and specific about the fix, is the strongest voice on the site. Keep it to that page and to posts signed by the owner. The hero, features and app stay in "you".

## Quotes

One per page at most, from Buffett, Housel or Clear, with the wording checked before print (B06, B10). They support the idea that habits compound; they are never the headline.

## Arabic

The Arabic pages are not a translation of the English ones: the same facts and screens, but the order, the examples and the words are shaped for an Egyptian reader. Polite Egyptian colloquial, short sentences, one idea each. No stiff fusha («يتيح لك»، «قم بـ»), no exaggeration, no exclamation marks. Fixed words: «التنزيل»، «جرّب ببلاش»، «المتاح للصرف»، «صافي ثروتك»، «الفلوس اللي خرجت»، «السجل»، «مهاب». App names come from the Arabic glossary. Speak to the person in neutral forms where a sentence allows: a verbal noun («اختيار الحساب»، «كتابة المبلغ») or a sentence without "you", not the masculine imperative («اختار»، «اكتب»); keep one spelling, and leave a form as it is when no neutral wording reads naturally. Money is «ج.م» after a figure in the app, ٪ after a percent.

## Two taglines, two jobs

- **All Your Money. One Clear Picture.** is the position: what the product does. It leads the home page and every banner.
- **Study Your Patterns. Control Your Future.** is the belief: why it matters. It signs off the page, under the mark.

Do not use them as interchangeable slogans, and do not add a third.

## Before you publish a line

1. Could this sentence sit in any other finance app? Rewrite it with a number, a place or a screen.
2. Is it true today, in the app as built?
3. Does it name or hint at another app? Cut it.
4. Is anything shouting: an exclamation mark, a deadline, a colour that means "alarm"? Calm it down.
5. Does a figure appear, and is it whole EGP, EGP once, with the working one tap away?
6. In Arabic: would an Egyptian friend say it out loud?
