  • Page header: the title, and under it the exact dates the numbers use (yyyy-mm-dd to yyyy-mm-dd), then a Muted note: “30 days”, “so far” for the current month, “year to date”. Never a slogan.
  • Period control: one track, four choices: All time, YTD, Month, Custom. Month carries its own ‹ › and stops at the current month. Custom shows its dates and opens the date range picker. Never a second pill bar beside it.
  • Page actions: one Secondary after the period control (Fill this month, Investment planner). A Primary only when it is the page’s main job. Anything else goes in ⋯. An icon stands alone only when its name is its tooltip and accessible label: the tab's gear and row actions.
  • One period, one source: the URL, the control and the line under the title always name the same dates; the page reads them from one place.
  • On a phone: see C04.
  • Header colours: Ink for titles, Ink 2 for the line under, Muted for dates. Never a meaning colour.

A06.2Arabic

  • Same sizes and roles. Readex Pro takes the place of Bricolage Grotesque (numbers, titles) and IBM Plex Sans Arabic the place of Manrope. Latin words inside a sentence (CIB, THNDR) keep the English faces.
  • Weights step down one: Readex Pro ends at 700, so 800 figures show at 700 and 700 titles at 600. Line height 1.75 for body, 1.3 for titles. No letter-spacing, no capitals, no italics.
  • Arabic-Indic digits with ٬ for thousands and ٫ for decimals. Field values and codes keep their own form. Dates stay yyyy-mm-dd and read left to right; a signed amount keeps its + or − in front.
  • The whole screen mirrors. Charts, time axes, numbers, dates and the logo do not. The full rules are A06.2 in app-ar.html.

A10Controls and fields

Pills to press. Soft wells to type in. Selected is always Nile.

A10.1Buttons

Add transactionReviewDeleteInvestment planner
  • Primary once per page · Secondary for the second action · Quiet inside cards · Danger only to delete. Labels start with a verb, no arrows. Row actions (delete, complete, restore) are icons named by their tooltip. One choice, one field: a segment picks the kind, and only that kind's field shows.

A10.2Choosing

  • Segment picks one of two to four. Chips filter. The month is a segment with its own ‹ › (A05), never “Monthly”; months open the month picker, never typed.

A10.6Working

Fetching prices…Fetching month-end prices…
  • A small Muted square that turns, never a circle; still when the user asks for less motion. It shows after 300 ms, so quick actions never flicker.
  • A pressed button becomes unavailable and says what it is doing. A slow page or work in the background shows the square in a note at the bottom right, which ends with what happened. The note sits above the page jump, which hides while it shows. The page jump is a quiet round button at the bottom right (bottom left in Arabic): it appears only when the pointer nears that corner or the page is scrolled, and fades 1.5 s later.

A10.7Search and Hide amounts

SearchCtrl K24,698
  • Search sits at the top of the sidebar as a soft well (A10.3's tint, 36 tall), its key hint at the end. It opens one dialog for pages, accounts, actions and #tags (A10.5).
  • Hide amounts is the eye beside it, a 36px icon button in the same well; pressed, it turns Nile. It is the one eye in the app and is named by its tooltip.
  • While amounts are hidden, every amount of money blurs 6px and money fields not being typed in blur 5px; percentages, labels, names and dates stay sharp. Pointing at an amount shows it. The choice is kept until pressed again. On a phone the eye sits in the app bar (C03.2).
