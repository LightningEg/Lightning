## Report
#626 (2026-11-13), parked for the owner's pick. Changes reach the user from separate places (Fill sources, Needs you, raise offer). A scheduled salary falling from 30,000 to 18,000 leaves the budget basis at 30,000 and `HealthService.raise_offer` offers nothing (raises only). A 2,400 shop on the 2nd against 3,000 is told it runs out around day 8; adding a 100 bill to that category turns pacing off for all of it. Next-month planning redirects back to this month.

## Fix
One dismissible review, only when something material changed (a changed bill, a new category with enough history, an unusual expense, sustained overspending, an income drop), each with its evidence and effective date; dismissals remembered. Pacing paces the flexible part of a category apart from its bills and speaks with confidence, not certainty. A read-only next-month preview is its own step. Reuse existing suggestions, schedules and averages; no second planning engine. Split with skill `split-large-item` when picked.
