## Report

Downloads is the site's busiest page after Home (GoatCounter since 2026-10-03: 19 English and 5 Arabic visits, about 14 downloads in all). On a phone it shows "Download for Windows" first and Android second, and is 6,163 px tall: release cards, the APK warning, sample data, the founder's essay ("What My Own Numbers Taught Me"), "Try Dendera" with unlock steps that 1.5.0 cannot do yet ("1.5.0 has previews only"), the feedback form, the survey and the email sign-up. The header button on this page is "Survey". The Arabic page has a "three steps" install section the English page lacks.

## Fix

Lead with the visitor's platform (Android on Android, Windows on Windows, both otherwise), then install steps, then sample data. Move the essay to How it works or Home, the theme block to Marketplace, and keep one way to give feedback. Give English the Arabic page's three install steps. Re-run the downloads funnel in GoatCounter two weeks after.
