## Report
Owner, 2026-10-31: comparisons are allowed with generic names, and must be honest with Low, Medium and High (convenience is not Lightning's strongest point yet). Guideline B10 and B12 currently say never to compare or mention other apps.

## Fix
One data file holds the scorecard (quality, comparator, rating, one line of evidence); the Competition doc, the comparison pages, `llms.txt` and `docs/STATUS.md` read it. Draft qualities: quick to log a spend, your data stays yours, wealth depth, Egypt and Arabic fit, numbers you can trace, easy to start.

## 8Z1 Scorecard and evidence rule · M
The data file and the rule that every rating cites a test, a Mohab step or a competition row; the owner signs off the ratings.

## 8Z2 Guideline B10 and B12 · M
Allow generic names and require Low, Medium, High with evidence; change both repos' `guideline/` the same session (`cmp` must pass).

## 8Z3 English pages · L
One page per comparator, built from the landing building blocks, with structured data.

## 8Z4 Arabic pages · L
Egyptian Arabic, hreflang, sitemap, `llms.txt`.
