## Report

[Store and community audit](../../reports/2026-10-27-store-and-community/report.md) M1–M6.

## Fix

- **Cart button always in reach:** a floating *Cart · 3* button (bottom end corner, safe-area aware, above the cookie bar) on Marketplace and Collection whenever the cart holds anything; it opens the cart window. Keep the chip in the wallet card. Each add says "Nile is in your cart" with *View cart*.
- **Cards:** one main action (*Add to cart*, or *Buy* when the cart is empty and the buyer prefers one step: owner picks; default *Add to cart* with *Buy now* in the window); *Try it* by pressing the preview; *Gift* and *Details* under one *More*. Owned: *Equipped*/*Wear*, *Get app code*, seats line.
- **One home per theme:** drop *Try these on your PC and phone*; each theme card that ships in the app carries an *On PC and phone* tag and its app note lives in Details. Replace the long *How to use Dendera* block with three steps that match the app (8L) and link Downloads.
- **Phone:** items lead. Hero to one line; *Your look* and wallet fold into one compact strip; the four promises move below the grid.
- **Cart window:** sentence case; a small swatch and kind per line; *Empty cart* moves to a quiet link; set suggestion (8N).
- Free Mono shows *Free* and *Wear*, never *Buy*.
- `?item=<id>` on marketplace.html scrolls to and highlights that item (the app's *See prices* link).
