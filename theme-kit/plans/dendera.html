The order of care

Function first, then clarity and polish, then looks

A theme is a material laid over Lightning, never a new layout. Every decision below was checked against that order: a texture or motion stays only if it costs no speed, sits under no reading and never competes with the figure for the eye. Where a look and a reading disagreed, the reading won.

Drawings

Four new recipes, now free for every theme

Each drawing is an SVG of plain shapes in lightning/ui/static/cosmetics/assets/dendera/. The build recolours it in the wearer's palette and inlines it, so nothing is fetched, and refuses links, scripts and images.

Zodiac ring zodiac-ring

Dendera's round zodiac: 36 decan stars between a double ring, a dial, and a sky of five-armed stars round the sun. The page's far corner, accent at 12%, mirrored in Arabic.

Winged sun disc winged-disc

The disc, two cobras and three rows of feathers, as over Dendera's doors. The hero card's empty corner, away from the figure, accent at 12%.

Lotus-bud frieze lotus-frieze

A 24 by 10 tile: a mefkat bud with a kenit heart, a desher dot, a lapis ground line. A header finish in the bottom padding, at 70%, 7 px tall on the PC and 5 px on the phone, under no text.

Pigment thread pigment-thread

The header's 1 px seam in lapis, kenit, mefkat and desher, with one soft light travelling it every 14 seconds. Only that strip repaints. Under reduced motion it is the still pigment seam.

Motion and speed

One motion that runs, two that answer, five refused

Runs

Pigment thread

  • 14 s loop along the header seam
  • One 1 px strip repaints
  • Still seam under reduced motion
Answers

When you act

  • Dialogs and sheets rise in, 0.32 s
  • One light sweep when you put the theme on, 1.2 s, never at launch
Refused

Cost more than they give

  • Seal press on every button
  • Rosette fade-in on Overview
  • A light that follows the pointer
  • Cards lifting on hover
  • Blur behind dialogs
