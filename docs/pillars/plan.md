# Plan

*Knowing what is safe to spend before payday.*

A budget by category, bills and loan payments, reserves for what is coming, and the next three months. Plans move no money; the ledger does.

**Deeper:** Architecture › Cash planning contract · Budget and reserve contract. How the pillars fit together: [the map](../README.md).

Choose **Paid for work** when recording an expense. Leave the expected reimbursement blank for the full expense, or enter the employer's share in the profile currency. In Budget, open **Paid for work** to see repayments and what was owed at period end. A repayment included in salary can be linked for just the reimbursed amount.
