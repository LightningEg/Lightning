# Egyptian institution logo files

Individual, real logos under assets (not a ZIP). Not generated by AI.

* \`icons/<id>.png\` — 256x256 PNG icon for account rows.
* \`wordmarks/<id>.png\` — 512x192 PNG with original proportions.
* \`originals/<id>.webp\` or \`.png\` — fetched source file.
* \`manifest.json\` — lookup of stable IDs and asset paths, including provenance.
* \`download-report.json\` — import status.

Examples: assets/institutions-logos/icons/cib.png, assets/institutions-logos/icons/nbe.png.

Original sources: https://banksegypt.com/banks (36 logos, third-party directory) and https://www.onebank.eg (onebank logo). Please check current branding and trademark permissions before a public release; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

## Egyptian wallets and fintech logos

This directory also includes Egyptian mobile wallets, savings apps, investment platforms and payment services, in the same `icons/`, `wordmarks/` and `originals/` structure. Images are authentic third-party marks, not AI-generated. The importer `tools/import_egypt_wallet_logos.py` fetches these from the named official sites (preferred) and a public Egyptian wallet image directory, normalizes them into PNGs, documents provenance in `manifest.json`, and copies selected 64 px files into `lightning/ui/static/institutions/` for the app's account picker. Wallet services, financial apps and banks are different categories; asset presence is not proof of an active provider or an endorsement. Trademarks remain with their owners.
