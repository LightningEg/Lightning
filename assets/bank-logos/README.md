# Egyptian bank logo files

Individual, real logos under assets (not a ZIP). Not generated by AI.

* \`icons/<id>.png\` — 256x256 PNG icon for account rows.
* \`wordmarks/<id>.png\` — 512x192 PNG with original proportions.
* \`originals/<id>.webp\` or \`.png\` — fetched source file.
* \`manifest.json\` — lookup of stable IDs and asset paths, including provenance.
* \`download-report.json\` — import status.

Examples: assets/bank-logos/icons/cib.png, assets/bank-logos/icons/nbe.png.

Original sources: https://banksegypt.com/banks (36 logos, third-party directory) and https://www.onebank.eg (onebank logo). Please check current branding and trademark permissions before a public release; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

## Additional digital wallets and fintech logos

The manifest also catalogs mobile wallets, payment services, and fintech platforms; not all of them are banks or e-wallets. Thndr is an investment platform, Money Fellows is a savings platform, InstaPay is a payment network and Fawry is a payment-services platform.

New assets are available as `icons/<id>.png` or `.svg`, with `wordmarks/<id>.*` where sourced and `originals/<id>.*` copies. SVG aspect ratios may differ from the original 256x256 bank PNGs: render with `object-fit: contain`. Source URLs and branding caveats are documented in the manifest. The Telda artwork is a third-party stylization and the Etisalat Cash artwork is legacy; verify against current official media kits before public release. Trademarks remain with their owners, and no endorsement is implied.
