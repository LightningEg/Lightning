# 2.3.2 · 2026-11-15

## Notes

- own: Target allocation is now set by risk level (low, medium, high), and you can move any holding to the level you want, so a money market fund counts toward your low-risk share.
- plan: Refilling the emergency fund or a goal by what the plan asks no longer lowers Safe to spend, and payday no longer offers the same saving twice.
- capture: Cash planning shows the upcoming calendar, refunds no longer give a 7,000% savings rate, and Settings has a Glossary.
- capture: Cash planning has no separate Upcoming tab; its calendar is on the Plan page.
- plan: Lightning can now remember a purchase you want budget averages to leave out.
- plan: Budget averages and Fill can leave out one purchase, with its refunds, while Cash flow and Spent still count it.
- plan: A Leave out of estimates switch on a purchase keeps one big buy from inflating your budget averages.
- plan: Moving budget between categories now asks whether to cover just this month or change your usual plan, so a December move no longer changes January unless you choose it.
- plan: Upcoming compares the same payments each month, Recurring shows the right income share, and bills can get a category when paid.
- plan: A refund can now name the purchase it returns, so budget averages count it in that purchase's month.
- plan: On a refund you can pick the purchase it returns, and a purchase can record its own refund.
- everywhere: Arabic names sort by their letters in recurring plans, held-money reports and name pickers.
- plan: Budget now shows next month as it will start, read only, so you can see your plan, bills and income before the month begins.
- plan: Running-out warnings now follow how you usually spend in a category and leave its bills aside, and say "may" until there are two months of history.
- plan: When your scheduled salary falls from a date, Budget offers to plan on the lower pay from then or keep your average.
- own: Certificate values stay at their recorded principal and cannot be overridden by ordinary price updates.
- plan: Cash planning links to budget and savings controls and offers payday funding after salary receipt.
- own: Total investment estimates now cover every account and gold item estimates update their actual value.
- plan: Compare scenarios now gives one answer, shows a late salary on the day it hurts, and a purchase you check can be added to your plan in one tap.
- plan: A side income before your salary now adds to Safe to spend instead of moving payday earlier.
- feel: The phone has a new card design on every screen, a Plan tab with what is due before payday, your bills, loans and what you set aside, and Activity now opens from Money.
- own: Sales entered by cash received now remove the correct number of investment units.
- own: Foreign investment purchase returns now match the holding value.
- own: Investment forms now show the currency you are entering.
- plan: You can now mark an investment, such as a fixed-income fund you can sell in days, to count in your free cash and safe to spend.
- feel: Messages after a save now go away by themselves after five seconds, and you can close one at once with its X.
- plan: Fill this month now opens on what actually changed, keeps the limits you set unless you choose otherwise, and says when nothing needs doing.
- plan: See and restore categories outside the budget on PC and phone.
- feel: Built-in categories use consistent sentence case.
- build: The Investments holdings table shows average cost and current price per unit.
- feel: Make English and Arabic wording more consistent.
- plan: Categories you leave out of the budget are now called Outside the budget, Budget says their spending still counts in Cash flow, and a limit set for this month only is marked as such.
- feel: Get an optional reminder when expected income is late.
- plan: Budget suggestions no longer treat one large purchase as a monthly cost, and money paid back counts against the month you spent it, so a refund can no longer make an average negative.
- feel: Edit headings show names and transaction details.
- everywhere: Application dates use one consistent clock.
- plan: The Move plan popup now says why a category needs room, instead of always claiming it was over its plan two months running.
- own: Workbook import now explains problems and shows a clearer review.
- feel: Papyrus and Dendera scroll smoothly on the phone and no longer keep the phone busy while it rests.
- feel: Scrolling on the phone does less work on every frame.
- everywhere: Date pickers now start the week on Saturday, matching the other calendars on PC and phone.
- feel: Saving on any page now keeps you where you are, without reloading; the transaction registers still reload for now.
